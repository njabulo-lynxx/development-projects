Mac and Cheese

This ultra-creamy, 20-minute stovetop mac and cheese uses a simple homemade cheese
sauce made from scratch. You only need everyday ingredients like macaroni, butter,
flour, milk, and real shredded cheese to make this comforting meal.

Ingredients:

  • Pasta: 1 box (225g / 8 oz) elbow macaroni
  • Butter: ¼ cup (60g) butter (salted or unsalted)
  • Flour: ¼ cup (30g) all-purpose flour
  • Milk: 2 cups (500ml) whole milk
  • Cheese: 2 cups (200g) shredded cheddar cheese (freshly grated melts best!)
  • Seasoning: Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Boil Pasta: Cook the macaroni in a large pot of salted boiling water according to package
    instructions until al dente (tender but firm). Drain and set aside.
  2. Make a Roux: In the same pot used for the pasta, melt the butter over medium heat. Whisk in
    the flour and cook for about 1 minute until it turns a light golden color and smells toasty.
  3. Thicken the Sauce: Gradually pour in the milk while whisking constantly to prevent lumps.
    Let the mixture bubble gently and thicken for about 5 minutes.
  4. Add Cheese: Remove the pot from the heat. Stir in the shredded cheese and seasonings until the
    sauce is completely melted, smooth, and creamy.
  5. Combine: Fold the cooked macaroni back into the pot, coating it entirely with the cheese sauce.
    Serve hot.

Pro-Tips:

  • Use Block Cheese: Pre-packaged shredded cheese contains anti-caking agents that make your sauce
    gritty. Grate your own cheese for a silky smooth finish.
  • Spice it up: Add a pinch of garlic powder, onion powder, or a dash of Dijon mustard to the sauce
    for extra flavor.
